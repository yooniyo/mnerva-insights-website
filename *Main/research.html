Research

Understanding
human systems.

Research and writing on how people, families, and organizations make decisions, navigate change, and sustain shared life across generations.

Our research agenda

Our interests span mindfulness and judgment, family systems, organizational behavior, power and negotiation, and succession across generations.

These questions connect our research agenda with the decisions families and organizations face in practice: how authority is exercised, how relationships shape cooperation, and how responsibility and meaning pass between generations.

Collaborative research

We are developing opportunities to work with researchers on questions relevant to our practice. Collaborations may include joint studies and exchanges between academic research and the experiences of families and organizations.

Original research

As the institute develops, we plan to build an internal research capacity and undertake original studies with our own researchers and external collaborators.

Projects and findings will be introduced here as the work develops.

Articles & perspectives

This section will also publish articles drawing on the work of researchers within and beyond MNerva. The aim is to make relevant findings, competing explanations, and open questions accessible to people making decisions in families and organizations.

Articles will distinguish the underlying research from our interpretation and its possible implications for practice.
