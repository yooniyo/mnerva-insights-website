A developing research program connecting scholars, practical questions, and partners who support the work.

Our questions

We are interested in how people, families, and organizations make decisions, navigate power and conflict, and carry responsibility and meaning across generations.

Our agenda connects mindfulness and judgment, family systems, organizational behavior, negotiation, and succession with the questions encountered in our practice.

Building research collaborations

mnerva will bring together researchers and practitioners around focused questions. We are developing opportunities for collaborative studies, commissioned research, and articles that connect existing scholarship with family and organizational life.

As the program grows, we aim to develop original research with external collaborators and, over time, an internal research team.

Partners who support the work

We are seeking funding partners to help develop selected research projects. mnerva will organize the questions and collaborations, with project scope and funding developed together with the people supporting the work.

Specific proposals and funding opportunities will be introduced as they take shape.

Research & writing

This space will publish research findings, articles informed by researchers' work, and perspectives on their implications for families and organizations.

Publications will be added as the work develops.
