The question behind our work

What allows a family
or institution to endure?

As families and organizations grow, the questions change. Who decides? How is power shared? How are differences resolved? What happens when leadership passes to someone new?

We work with founders, family members, rising generations, and organizational leaders to give those questions a workable structure.

That means attending to the person, the relationships between people, and the arrangements that hold a system together over time.

How we work with clients

Across generations

A shared future.
A meaningful life
for each generation.

For founders and the first two generations, we help build awareness of their roles, plan personal growth and succession, navigate family relationships, and prepare for major life transitions.

For the third generation and beyond, we help people find their own value and direction, and shape a meaningful relationship between the lives they choose and the family they belong to.

Our work connects individual development with the responsibilities, relationships, and purposes of the wider family.

Family & next-generation advisory

People. Systems. Environments.

Durable governance depends on people who can remain present, exercise judgment, and stay in relationship under stress.

We attend to the balance within each person, the relationships and structures of the group, and the environments in which they live and work. These levels continually influence one another; sound governance helps them adapt together.
