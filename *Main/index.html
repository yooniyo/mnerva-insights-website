The question behind our work

What allows a family
or institution to endure?

As families and organizations grow, the questions change. Who decides? How is power shared? How are differences resolved? What happens when leadership passes to someone new?

We work with founders, family members, rising generations, and organizational leaders to give those questions a workable structure.

That means attending to the person, the relationships between people, and the arrangements that hold a system together over time.

How we work with clients

Structures. People. Continuity.

Durable governance depends on people who can remain present, exercise judgment, and stay in relationship under stress.

Our work brings rules, roles, and decision rights together with the human capacities needed to put them into practice.
