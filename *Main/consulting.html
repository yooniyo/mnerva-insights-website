Advisory

A longer view.
A partner in the work.

Ongoing guidance for the decisions, relationships, and transitions that shape family and organizational life.

Family Governance

Roles, decision rights, family agreements, ownership and equity allocation. We help clarify how authority and responsibility are shared, including plans for succession.

Rising Generation

Personal direction, education, practical experience, and readiness for responsibility. Finding a meaningful place within the family, the enterprise, or a path of one’s own.

Relationships & Family Meetings

Understanding relationship dynamics, facilitating difficult conversations, and designing family meetings with clear purposes and meaningful participation.

Life Transitions

Support through changing family roles, marriage, reproductive decisions, early parenting, caregiving, aging, and loss.

Neurodiversity & Disability in Family Life

Communication, participation, autonomy, and support for neurodivergent and disabled family members. Building arrangements that respect different needs and ways of contributing.

Purpose & Philanthropy

Connecting personal and family values with meaningful contribution. Clarifying priorities, participation, and decision processes for family giving.

Family Enterprise & Organizations

Organizational design, team development, incentives, culture, and conflict. Connecting the structures of the enterprise with the people who make them work.

Working Together

We begin by understanding your situation and making evidence visible. Together, we define priorities, develop practical arrangements, and revisit them as circumstances change.
