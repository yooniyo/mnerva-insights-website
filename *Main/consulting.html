Practice

Governance Consulting

Strategic advice for businesses, families, and family enterprises.

Understand the system

We research how enterprises and families function: how decisions are made, how people work together, where power sits, and where formal structures diverge from everyday practice.

Make evidence visible

We analyze and present evidence so clients can see the relationships, choices, and trade-offs before them. Our work helps clarify the questions that need decisions and the interests those decisions must take into account.

Design practical changes

We translate findings into strategic recommendations for family management, leadership, agreements, ownership, and organizational structures.

Areas of Advice

Family Governance & Management

Roles, decision rights, accountability, family agreements, and coordination among family members and the enterprise.

Succession & Continuity

Leadership transitions, next-generation preparation, and the transfer of authority and responsibility across generations.

Ownership & Equity Allocation

Ownership strategy, equity allocation, and the relationship between contribution, responsibility, and decision-making power.

Teams & Organizational Behavior

Team development, organizational design, collaboration, incentives, culture, and conflict.

Next-Generation Education & Development

Individual guidance on educational direction, learning experiences, mentoring, practical exposure, and readiness for responsibility. We help families connect a young person's interests and strengths with a realistic plan for growth.

This can include preparation to enter the family enterprise, finding a role beyond it, and clarifying how personal aspirations relate to family expectations. Plans can evolve as the person grows.

Family Relationships & Life Transitions

Guidance for conversations about changing family roles, parenthood, aging, loss, succession, and other major transitions. We help families clarify expectations, responsibilities, and the decisions they need to make together.

Identity, Purpose & Contribution

For later generations, we help explore individual direction, sources of personal value, and meaningful ways to contribute within and beyond the family. Personal purpose and shared family purpose are developed in conversation with one another.

Reproductive Decisions & Early Parenting

Consulting on decisions about whether, when, and how to become parents, preparation for family change, and the arrangements that support early parenting.

We help clarify priorities, caregiving roles, the division of responsibilities, support needs, and expectations between partners and generations.
