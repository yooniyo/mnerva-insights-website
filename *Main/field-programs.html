Field Programs

A different setting.
A deeper conversation.

Travel and shared experiences create space to step outside familiar roles, encounter different perspectives, and consider how we live and decide together.

People, systems & environments

A change of setting can change the pace and quality of attention people bring to one another. Our field programs consider individual presence, the dynamics of the group, and the conditions around them together.

We work toward balance within each level and a more workable relationship between them: room for rest and self-regulation, room for honest conversation, and a setting that supports both.

Agreements about ownership, roles, and succession take shape within human relationships. How people understand and carry out those agreements depends in part on trust, communication, and their capacity to work through disagreement.
