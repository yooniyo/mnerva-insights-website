Family Governance Retreats

Shared experience creates room for new perspectives, focused conversation, and time together with attention.

A different setting for family conversations

We design yacht-based retreats for one or two families, bringing questions of governance, responsibility, and succession into shared experience.

Facilitated conversations create space to consider how a family makes decisions, navigates differences, and prepares for its next chapter.

Presence, rest & body awareness

Time on board also includes space for rest, body awareness, and one-to-one biodynamic craniosacral work.

These practices sit alongside the conversations, making room for individual presence within the work of living and deciding together.

Learning through travel

Our field programs use travel to open up unfamiliar perspectives and bring people into contact with different ways of living and organizing human life.

The setting supports shared learning and sustained attention to the questions participants bring.
