Family Succession

Our courses explore how families prepare for transitions, carry forward shared purposes, and make room for the next generation to exercise judgment and responsibility.

Power & Negotiation

We examine how power operates in families and institutions, how competing interests are negotiated, and how people build agreements they can uphold.

Birth, Death & Generational Responsibility

Generational continuity also involves decisions about reproduction, parenthood, death, and what people choose to pass on. These topics form part of our broader educational work on family life and responsibility.
