MNerva Insights
Retreats

A gathering that celebrates the insights and wisdom of women who lead families and women who become part of them.

Wisdom worth sharing

Each woman brings a history, perspective, and capacity for judgment of her own. These retreats make room for that wisdom to be recognized, exchanged, and brought into the life of the family.

Room for attention

Retreats bring together rest, shared experience, and facilitated conversation, creating space to notice personal needs and reflect on familiar roles and expectations.

Relationships, transitions & direction

Conversations explore family dynamics, changing responsibilities, personal development, and choices about how to live and contribute within and beyond the family.
